FreeFlow audio lab script
Every name, address and number below is invented.

Every line comes twice:
  SAY: what you read aloud. Words in parentheses are instructions: do not read them.
  REF: the reference text for that line, written the way Deepgram's smart_format
       prints it: digits, times like 6:30, money like $18,500, emails like
       priya@example.com.

Read the SAY lines of a take in order, with a short pause between lines. The
take's reference .txt file holds the REF text of the same lines, in order, one
per line, without the "REF:" label and the line number. Punctuation and case
do not matter for the main WER; the words, and how numbers are written, do.
The REF forms of numbers, dates, times, emails and URLs are smart_format's
likely output: check them against a first small run with --save-transcripts
and fix only how such items are written, never which words you said.
